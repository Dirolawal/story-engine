# Story Bible — Chris

*The full life story. Pulled from the May 16–17 onboarding calls (Fathom). Use this when scripting any storytelling content. Every piece of content should be rooted in something on this page.*

## Identity Snapshot

- **Age:** 29
- **Location:** Bristol, UK
- **Heritage:** Mum is South African (came to UK aged 11–14). Dad is English. Granddad on mum's side was Northern Irish.
- **Ancestry flex:** Directly descended from William Wallace (Braveheart). Real.
- **Tattoo to remember:** *Amor Fati* on forearm — Nietzsche's "love of fate." Catchphrase.
- **Brain:** Slight eidetic (photographic) memory. IQ tested at 129 aged 11 (gifted & talented register).
- **Self-description:** Architect of his identity. Cracked-out shaman + warrior. Trickster archetype — rebellious creator who breaks systems and shows a better way.

## Chapter 1 — The Wimpy Kid (0–11)

- Born Basingstoke Hospital (South England, Hampshire/Berkshire area).
- **Moved 8+ times as a kid.** Mum was running from his dad — abusive, threatening, following them. They drifted northwest: Devizes → Newbury → Marlborough.
- **Father gone since age 2.** Took £10K from mum's account when he left. No child support, ever. Mum never claimed benefits.
- Money was tight. Mum skipped meals so Chris could eat. Lied she "wasn't hungry."
- **Two weird schools shaped his brain:**
  - Newbury — 8 kids total across Years 1–6, all in one room. Taught ancient Rome, Egypt, Greek city-states. Trips to Roman forts and museums aged 7–8.
  - Marlborough — head teacher was a fantasy novelist (high-fantasy, dragons). Once a week, kids did creative writing in his fictional world.
- **One friend** from Newbury — a French kid called Louis. Then no friends for years.
- Read constantly: mythology, history, encyclopedias. Greek mythology "imprinted" from a young age.
- Played alone outside, read books, played Warhammer.
- "Home is not a place. I had 10 childhood homes. Home is your space."
- Mum took him traveling — Tunisia 6–7 times during the Stefan years. Took him to libraries, theatres. Made him consume culture.

## Chapter 2 — Stefan, The Stepfather (~7–11)

- Mum married an Algerian man called Stefan.
- Chris loved him. "Nicest fucking guy. Incredible person."
- **The reveal — Chris only found out a year ago:**
  - Stefan was trafficked into the UK by a gang. They held his passport.
  - The marriage to Mum was fabricated by the gang for citizenship. Mum thought it was real.
  - Stefan eventually started running the same operation — flying to Pakistan, bringing people in, taking their passports.
  - **Mum went undercover with the police.** Helped them bust the ring. Took years.
  - Chris thought they had just broken up. Didn't know any of this until ~2025.
- Stefan also took a load of mum's money to pay off the gang. She's still in debt because of it.

## Chapter 3 — The Dad Returns

- Dad came back into Chris's life around the Stefan years.
- Tattooed, racist, hooligan-type. Nigel Farage's Britain. Bullied Chris for being sensitive.
- Tried to push him into football and rugby. Chris wanted Lord of the Rings and Star Wars.
- Made Chris feel weak, wimpy, unmanly. Every reactive change later in life ties back to here.
- **Reaching out to dad in May 2026 — first contact in ~20 years.** Doing it to heal the inner child and see the man behind the myth.

## Chapter 4 — Granddad's Death (~15)

- Mum's dad. Northern Irish. Massive role model.
- Died at 65 (cancer-ish, fast).
- The moment that changed him: in hospital, granddad grabbed his hand. "I'm scared to die. I don't want to die. Help me."
- Chris saw the mask come off. Saw the vulnerable human behind the character.
- "He never got to see me become a man. The only legacy left is my memory of him. I don't want to be a fading memory."

## Chapter 5 — The Triple Threat (14–16)

Three things happened around the same year and rewrote him:

1. **Read Nietzsche and Camus' *The Stranger*** at 14–15. "Holy shit, you can be whoever you want. You can create your reality from this moment."
2. **Got bulimic.** Was a chubby kid, then swung the other way. Sit-ups and push-ups every night after mum slept. HIIT twice a day. <5% body fat for a year. Threw up any meal with carbs.
3. **First LSD at 15.** Saw the sky shimmer, fractals. "This is a simulation. I can influence reality." Matrix moment.

Also at 15: lost his virginity, started the gym properly, got into philosophy. Switched from observer to operator. Started experimenting with how to influence people, how to look, how to talk.

## Chapter 6 — School Trickster (15–18)

- Druggie kid at school who was *also* gifted & talented.
- Did an A-Level (Politics) at GCSE age.
- Came into school high. Drank at school. Got into Bristol Uni.
- Was the chaotic-but-smart one. Mum was constantly challenged by him. Classic rebellion.

## Chapter 7 — Bristol Uni: Pure Hedonism (18–21)

- Studied Politics and International Relations.
- Acid 1–2x/week. Mandy weekly. DMT.
- **One heroic dose:** 4g shrooms + ~1.5 tabs (~400ug) acid in one sitting. 20 hours high. Total ego death. Saw himself as a statue and broke it. Read his friend's thoughts. Saw the "code" of reality — sacred geometry. Watched 20 real hot air balloons rise over Bristol as the sun came up. Religious experience.
- **DMT:** sacred geometric temples with fractal heads, snakes, cosmic entities whispering "this is not real, there is something much bigger on the other side."
- "Why do I care about a degree? Why do I care about a job?"
- **At the same time — became Junior British Powerlifting Champion.** Held British bench record: **112.5kg at 57kg bodyweight** — double bodyweight bench.
- Current lifetime PBs: **140kg bench, 190kg squat, 235kg deadlift, 100kg snatch, 130kg clean & jerk.** Real athlete. Competed in 4 different sports.
- Disillusioned by politics studies. Dropped out of a masters.

## Chapter 8 — The Bar Years / Alcoholism (~22–26)

- Bartender → bar manager → sommelier (formal wine quals). Opened and ran a rum bar.
- **Became an alcoholic.** Drinking from waking to sleeping for 3–4 years.
- Bottle of rum a day at work. Functional. Talking to customers, hitting his Pour, dying inside.
- Dealt drugs for gangs at points.
- "I only felt like I existed when I was drunk or on drugs."
- Became a caricature — the fun guy at the party. Inconsistent, unreliable, a story other people told about him.

## Chapter 9 — The Worst Day (PROTECTED — see no-go.md)

- ~age 19–20, second year Bristol.
- A fitness photographer drugged him with a spiked whiskey & coke. Got out, ran shoeless to a petrol station, knocked on doors trying to wake people up. Police picked him up — eyes rolling — and the perp claimed to be his friend. Police drove him *back to the perp's house*.
- Six months later, two detectives at his door: the man had been arrested as a serial rapist. 30+ male victims. One had killed himself. Chris was the first to ever report him.
- Chris's case forced a national change in UK policing policy on next-day medical referrals. The anonymous Sky News interviewee = him.
- That night: Chris stood on the edge of Clifton Suspension Bridge. The only time he ever genuinely considered ending it.
- **The Boomtown statement** — at the court case, he was high on acid at the festival. Wrote a statement read aloud: *"Right now you're hearing this. I'm at Boomtown. You will be insignificant to me, and you will go away for a very long time and have a very nasty time in prison while I go on living my life like you never happened."*
- **NOT for content yet.** Chris said so. May be usable later in a deeply controlled, deliberate piece — only with his sign-off. The lesson he extracted is content-safe: *"Stop putting yourself in vulnerable positions. Start putting yourself in positions of power."*

## Chapter 10 — Lost Everything Day (~25–26)

- Cheated on his girlfriend. 3–4 years later, his boss drunkenly told her to break them up.
- **Lost his girlfriend, his house, and his job — all on the same day.**
- "I had to start a new fucking life."

## Chapter 11 — Becoming a PT (~26–29)

- Decided to double down on the thing he thought about 70% of every day — fitness, self-improvement.
- Took the risk. Went all in.
- "I don't want to be a wasteman. I don't want to be the fun guy at the party. I want to be the guy people go, *how the fuck did he do that?*"
- Built a PT practice. 50 clients. Strong in-person retention (1yr+, some 3–4 years).
- 2 × £10K months last year. First time. Big proof point.

## Chapter 12 — Now (May 2026)

- Building the personal brand properly with Diro.
- Repositioning from PT → **Transformation Coach.**
- Writing a book/framework on the side using AI — the *mastery* book. Title direction: *"Icarus laughed as he fell"* — he got to see reality from a height no one else could.
- Brand framework forming: **SOVEREIGNTY** — mastery of mind, body, action, destiny.
- Travels 6x a year. Caribbean, Kazakhstan, Thailand, Bali on the docket.
- Reaching out to dad after 20 years.
- Still has anxiety and insomnia. Has had ADHD, addiction, burnout — never clinically depressed.
